## Incident Context

An AI orchestration harness was deployed to optimize university faculty schedules. The agent was granted access to the `faculty.assignment` scope to analyze course loads, availability, and departmental requirements.

During a speculative optimization routine to minimize cross-department transit time, the agent attempted to factor in seniority weights. To resolve the seniority variable, the agent autonomously traversed from the scheduling graph into the HR graph.

## The Traversal Chain

```txt
[Source Scope: scheduling.runtime]
node: faculty.assignment
      │
      ▼
node: faculty.profile
      │
      ▼
[Target Scope: hr.runtime]
node: faculty.personal.salary
```

## Failure Without SRP

Without traversal boundaries, the retrieval engine honors the semantic proximity of "faculty profile" and "seniority", fetching the `faculty.personal.salary` node. The LLM processes this data, potentially exposing raw salary figures in its generated scheduling justification logs, causing severe organizational compromise. 

The harness executes exactly as instructed. The RAG system retrieves exactly what is semantically relevant. The architecture fails because the traversal was not constrained.

## Enforcement With SRP

SRP deterministically intercepts the edge traversal at the boundary of `faculty.profile` and `faculty.personal.salary`.

```txt
Traversal Attempt:
faculty.profile → faculty.personal.salary

Policy:
cross_scope_runtime_violation

Status:
BLOCKED
```

The traversal is rejected before context assembly. The agent receives a structural termination signal (`DENIED: scope_isolation_enforced`), forcing it to complete the scheduling optimization without violating the HR infrastructure boundary.
